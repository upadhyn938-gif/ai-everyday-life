How Artificial Intelligence Is Changing Everyday Life

Understanding Artificial Intelligence

Artificial intelligence, commonly known as AI, is technology that allows computers to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence. Today, AI is becoming a regular part of everyday life.

From recommending movies to helping people navigate traffic, AI works behind many of the digital tools we use every day. It can analyze information, recognize patterns, and provide useful suggestions.

Common Uses of AI

  • Virtual assistants
  • Movie and music recommendations
  • Navigation and traffic predictions
  • Online shopping recommendations
  • Spam and fraud detection
