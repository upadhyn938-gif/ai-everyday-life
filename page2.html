AI Around Us

Where We Use AI Every Day

Many people use artificial intelligence every day without realizing it. AI can be found in smartphones, streaming services, navigation apps, online shopping, and many other technologies.

AI can make everyday tasks faster and more convenient. However, it is also important for people to understand how these systems use data and how their recommendations can influence the choices we make.

Examples of AI in a Typical Day

  1. Using facial recognition to unlock a smartphone
  2. Checking a navigation app for the fastest route
  3. Receiving personalized recommendations online
  4. Using a chatbot to ask questions
  5. Watching recommended videos or movies
